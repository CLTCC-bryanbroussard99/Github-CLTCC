Records & Registration

Manage course schedules, program changes, degree verification, and academic status records. Please allow a minimum of 3 to 5 business days for student records, registration changes, or transcript requests to be fully processed. Ensure all signatures and required attachments are included prior to submission to prevent delays.

Submit Student Drop / Withdrawal Form

Initiate a request for assistance with dropping or withdrawing from a course.
Standard class drops can also be completed independently in LoLA (Log On Louisiana, the online student portal).

Request Administrative Withdrawal

Request an administrative withdrawal after the published institutional withdrawal deadline due to extenuating circumstances.

Submit Administrative Drop / Delete Form

Submit an administrative drop or deletion request when directed by an Academic Dean or Enrollment Staff member.

Request Course Substitution / Waiver

Request approval to substitute or waive a required course based on prior coursework, equivalent experience, or demonstrated competency.

Apply for Prior Learning Credit

Apply for college-level credit earned through professional work experience, military service, industry certifications, or external training.

Submit Graduation Application

Apply for degree or certificate completion and verify completion status prior to the commencement deadline.

Access Incomplete Grade Request Contract

Establish a formal contract with an instructor to complete coursework after term end due to emergency circumstances.

Request Prior College Transcript Evaluation

Have official transcripts from prior accredited institutions evaluated for transfer credit toward your active CLTCC program.

Submit Program Change Request Form

Change your major or program of study. Requires authorization signatures from corresponding academic program advisors.

Request Proof of Enrollment Verification

Obtain official documentation of current or past enrollment status for employers, insurance providers, military, or loan deferment.

Review FERPA Authorization & Privacy Notice

Required Documentation

Complete Identification Verification Form

Verify identity prior to enrollment completion. Submit in person with a valid government-issued ID at any campus or mail a notarized form to Admissions.

Submit Immunization Compliance Records

Submit proof of required immunizations

  • Measles, Mumps, Rubella (MMR) – Two doses
  • Tetanus, Diphtheria (Td/Tdap) – One dose within the last ten years
  • Meningococcal – Two doses OR one dose if first dose was given on or after age 16
  • or process exemption forms directly through LoLA.

Verify Selective Service Registration

Required for male applicants aged 18 to 25. Provide proof of compliance or submit electronic compliance forms in LoLA.

Supplemental Documentation

Submit High School Transcript, GED, or HiSET

While high school completion is not required for general admission, it is required for entry into all Nursing and Allied Health programs except for Nurse Assistant. High school completion is also required to be eligible to receive financial aid.

Submit Placement Test Scores (ACT / Accuplacer)

ACT scores can be requested by visiting the ACT website and entering school code: 5015.
Accuplacer (Math, Reading, and English) is offered at each CLTCC Campus location. 
Please note: Practical Nursing has REQUIRED test scores for acceptance into the program. Please visit your campus coordinator for more information.
Please see Placement Testing for more information. 

Submit Official College Transcripts

Request previous institutions to send official electronic transcripts directly to transcripts@cltcc.edu or via sealed mail.
