Procedure Checklist

  1. Measure and cut the CAT6 cable to the specified length.
  2. Slide the boot covers onto the cable before stripping the jacket.
  3. Strip the outer jacket (approx. 1 inch), ensuring no copper conductors are nicked.
  4. Untwist and arrange wires according to the T568B standard.
  5. Trim wires to an even length (approx. 0.5 inches).
  6. Insert wires into the RJ45 plug and crimp using the authorized tool.
  7. Verify functionality using the cable tester.

Lab Submission & Assessment Criteria

Before submitting your CAT6 patch cables for grading, use this checklist to ensure your work meets professional requirements.

1. Physical Construction Standards

  • Jacket Retention: The cable jacket must extend inside the RJ45 connector and be held firmly by the crimp-down strain relief tab. If you can see individual twisted pairs outside the connector, the termination is rejected.
  • Boot Placement: The snagless boot must be fully seated over the connector to provide proper cable support.
  • Flush Trimming: All eight conductors must be cut flush and reach the very end of the RJ45 pins to ensure a solid connection.

2. Wiring Standard (TIA/EIA-568B)

The sequence must match the T568B standard exactly from left to right when holding the connector with the gold pins facing up and the clip facing away.

Sequence (Left to Right): White/Orange, Orange, White/Green, Blue, White/Blue, Green, White/Brown, Brown.

3. Electrical Performance Testing

Every cable must pass a continuity and wire-map test using a digital cable tester.

  • Continuity: Every pin (1-8) must show a solid connection without intermittent flickering.
  • Wire Map: The tester must confirm a "Straight-Through" connection. Any "Crossed Pair" or "Split Pair" results indicate a failure.
  • Short Circuits: The tester must show no shorts between adjacent pins.

Submission Checklist

The cable is verified for T568B standard. All connectors pass continuity testing (1-8). Jacket is seated inside the connector housing. Snagless boots are installed and secure.
