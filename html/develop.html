Required: Crash Course in Python Microcredential

Why This Matters

The Crash Course in Python microcredential is a required part of this course. A microcredential is a verified digital recognition that demonstrates completion of a specific skill or learning experience.

You must submit proof of this microcredential to receive course credit. Completing the course without submitting the required certificate information may result in the assignment being marked incomplete.

Before You Submit

After completing the Crash Course in Python microcredential, follow every step below. You will submit both required URLs directly in the Canvas assignment submission text box. These links allow your instructor to verify your completion and certificate.

Step 1: Click on your Profile Icon located in the top-right corner of your dashboard.
Step 2: Select Completed from the navigation bar.
Step 3: Locate the course and click View Certificate.
Step 4: Click the Share Certificate button.
Step 5: Copy the public link provided and paste both URLs directly into the Canvas assignment submission text box below.

Final Check

Before submitting, confirm that you have:

✓ Completed the Crash Course in Python microcredential.
✓ Opened and verified your certificate.
✓ Copied the required public certificate link.
✓ Pasted both URLs into the Canvas submission text box.

Remember: This submission is required for course credit. Do not skip these steps.
