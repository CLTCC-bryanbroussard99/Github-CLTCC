Important Notice: Accessing Your uCertify Coursework

Updated Integration Process

The technical link integration between Canvas LMS and uCertify has been updated. Each individual assignment, lab, and quiz in Canvas now links directly to its corresponding content in uCertify.

Access Restricted Notice

If you attempt to navigate within uCertify directly or open external bookmarks, you may see a restriction screen similar to the image below. Do not be concerned if this appears. This is an intended security measure confirming that direct external access is disabled.

Standard Access Restricted screen displayed when bypassing Canvas SSO.

How to Complete Your Assignments

1. Launch directly from Canvas: You must click the specific assignment link located inside your weekly Canvas modules to access your material via Single Sign-On (SSO).
2. Avoid direct uCertify bookmarks: Navigating directly to the external uCertify website triggers the access block above and prevents your grades from syncing to Canvas.
3. Grade Verification: Launching from Canvas establishes a secure connection so your lab and quiz scores immediately write back to the Canvas gradebook.

So What? Why This Matters to You

This direct link structure ensures your work is properly authenticated and your grades transfer seamlessly. Simply return to Canvas, click your required assignment link, and you will regain instant access to your coursework.

If you experience technical issues launching coursework, navigate to the Canvas Modules Index Page to ensure you are launching from an active assignment link, or contact technical support.
