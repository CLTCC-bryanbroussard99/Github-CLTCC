1. Grants (Free Money)

Grants are financial awards provided by federal and state governments that do not require repayment. They may be need-based, merit-based, or student-specific. Major programs include:

  • Federal Pell Grants: Need-based grants for undergraduate students.
  • Federal Supplemental Educational Opportunity Grant (FSEOG): For undergraduate students with exceptional financial need.
  • State Grants: Programs like the Louisiana Go Grant and MJ Foster Promise.

2. Scholarships

Scholarships are gift aid awarded based on academic performance, technical skills, community involvement, or specific career paths. CLTCC students can apply for institutional and external awards such as:

  • Alexandria Business Foundation Scholarship
  • American Welding Society Scholarships
  • Cleco Power of a Promise Scholarship
  • TOPS and TOPS Tech Awards

3. Federal Work-Study

Federal work-study provides part-time employment opportunities for eligible undergraduate students, allowing them to earn money to help pay for educational expenses while gaining valuable work experience on campus.

4. Student Loans

Loans are borrowed funds that must be repaid with interest. CLTCC encourages students to maximize federal and state grants and scholarships before considering loans. For complete details, review the official CLTCC Types of Aid Page.
