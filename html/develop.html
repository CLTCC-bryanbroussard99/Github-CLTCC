Purchasing Books

Please follow these instructions carefully to purchase and access your cybersecurity course materials.

Important: You should ONLY purchase the required books for THIS SEMESTER, which includes A Term & B term for Fall and Spring Semesters.  The information below includes required books for the ENTIRE PROGRAM, this semester, is available below..

Important: Always use your official school email address when creating accounts to ensure synchronization.

Method A: Using a School Voucher

Follow these steps if you are utilizing school-issued financial aid vouchers.

  1. Access Bookstore: Visit the Official Bookstore Portal. Select the "Use It Here" button.
  2. Authentication:
    • Student ID: Your LoLA ID (e.g., L12345678)
    • Voucher ID: Format is SemesterYear (e.g., SPRING2026)
  3. Filter Search:
    • Term: Current Semester
    • Site: Technical Programs
    • Course ID: Cybersecurity
  4. Checkout: Add the textbook to your cart and complete the order.

Method B: Out-of-Pocket Purchase

Follow these steps if you are paying directly via credit card or PayPal.

  1. Access Bookstore: Visit the Official Bookstore Portal. Select the "Let's Get Started" button.
  2. Filter Search:
    • Term: Current Semester
    • Site: Technical Programs
    • Course ID: Cybersecurity
  3. Finalize: Locate the required textbook, add to cart, and complete payment.
