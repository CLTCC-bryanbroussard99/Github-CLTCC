Student Guide: Using Your Book Voucher

Central Louisiana Technical Community College • Financial Aid & Course Materials

Overview & Virtual Bookstore

CLTCC partners with MBS Direct/Barnes & Noble College (BNC) to operate our virtual bookstore. Eligible students can use Financial Aid book vouchers to purchase required textbooks and online lab access codes directly through MBS Direct.

Voucher Eligibility Requirements

A book voucher is an advance authorization that allows you to charge textbook purchases against your pending financial aid before it pays out.

The Financial Aid office automatically approves vouchers for students who meet both conditions:

• You have awarded Federal Financial Aid for the semester.
• Your total aid exceeds your current tuition and fees, leaving a projected credit balance.
