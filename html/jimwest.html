This is Jim West's "giving back to the profession" initiative and he aims to help as many students as he can each and every year. He has helped over a hundred thousand people pass their exams and go on to six figure careers across hius 35 year career.
His presentation goes over his masterclass in certification exams. It lasts 45 minutes to an hour.
